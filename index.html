PAUSE — Care, with CARE+ as extended scope

Nothing gets reopened until it’s actually ready.

Care is the heart of PAUSE — fixing and checking things properly. CARE+ isn’t a separate step after REPLAY; it’s the ongoing support inside Care that keeps things working well long after the first fix.

A trusted partnering ecosystem behind every Care cycle
